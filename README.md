## 📌 About the Internship

This internship focused on providing remedial academic support to students of
Classes I, III and V at the Mahila SewaShakti Foundation centre. Sessions ran
weekly, two hours each, over one month — covering mathematics (counting,
tables, multiplication & division) and language/reading skills, alongside
building the intern's own communication, patience and teamwork.

**Organization:** Mahila SewaShakti Foundation — an NGO working towards the
welfare, empowerment and education of underprivileged communities, with a
focus on women and children.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Conduct engaging and effective teaching sessions for Classes I, III & V
- Assess existing academic level of each student and identify learning gaps
- Help students improve basic reading, writing and mathematical skills
- Build student confidence and encourage active participation
- Work collaboratively as a group of interns under NGO mentorship
- Develop soft skills — patience, communication, empathy, responsibility

---

## 🗓️ Week-wise Curriculum

| Week | Focus | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orientation & Baseline Assessment | Assessed existing reading, writing and number skills; basic counting and place value (1000–1500 range) |
| 2 | Combined Remedial Teaching | Mental maths (multiplication & division), Hindi reading practice |
| 3 | Group Teaching Across Classes | Table learning (14–19), word problems, handwriting practice |
| 4 | Review, Consolidation & Assessment | Doubt-clearing, support for weaker students, small oral tests |
